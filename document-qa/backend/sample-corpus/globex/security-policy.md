# Globex Security Policy

All employees must enable multi-factor authentication on their work accounts. Passwords must be at least 14 characters long and must not be reused.

## Devices

Company laptops must use full-disk encryption and install security patches within 7 days of release. Lost or stolen devices must be reported to IT security within one hour.

## Access reviews

Managers review access to production systems every quarter and remove access that is no longer needed.
