# Expense Reimbursement Policy

Acme reimburses reasonable business expenses that employees pay out of pocket. This policy applies to all full-time and part-time staff.

## Submitting a claim

Submit expense claims in the finance portal within 30 days of the purchase. Claims submitted after 30 days need written approval from your manager and the finance team. Attach an itemized receipt for every expense over 25 dollars.

## Meals and entertainment

Meals while travelling are reimbursed up to 75 dollars per day. Client entertainment requires the names of all attendees and the business purpose. Alcohol is not reimbursed unless a client is present.

## Travel

Book flights and hotels through the company travel desk at least 14 days before the trip. Economy class is the default for flights under six hours. Mileage for a personal car is reimbursed at the published federal rate.

## Approval and payment

Your manager approves claims under 1,000 dollars; larger claims also need a director. Approved claims are paid with the next payroll run, usually within two weeks.
